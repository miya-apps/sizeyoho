# Guide URL conventions

Public guide URLs use short English slugs under a topic directory.

- Base: `/sizeyoho/guide/`
- Child clothing: `/sizeyoho/guide/clothes/`
- Child shoes: `/sizeyoho/guide/shoes/`
- Use lowercase ASCII words separated by hyphens.
- Do not mix romanized Japanese words such as `kodomo`, `fuku`, `kutsu`, `nansai`, or `hayamihyo` into new public URLs.
- When a published URL changes, keep the old path as a redirect page and list only the new canonical URL in `docs/sitemap.xml`.
